Delete Your Anxious Attachment Companion Data

Anxious Attachment Companion is published and operated by AB Publisher LLC.

You may delete some or all of your app data without deleting your Anxious Attachment Companion account.

Delete private data stored on your device

Your check-ins, trigger records, assessments, message drafts, history, and similar private reflections are primarily stored locally on your device.

You can remove this locally stored data while keeping your account.

  1. Open Anxious Attachment Companion.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Use the available Clear All Data option.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

You may also remove local app data by clearing the app's storage through your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Request deletion of account-associated backend data

If you want certain account-associated backend data deleted while keeping your account active, you may send a request to AB Publisher LLC.

  1. Send the request from the email address associated with your Anxious Attachment Companion account.
  2. Send it to support@abpublisherllc.com.
  3. Use the subject line: Anxious Attachment Companion - Data Deletion Request.
  4. Clearly describe the data you want us to delete while keeping your account active.

Do not send your password. We will never ask you to provide your account password in a data deletion request.

Request Data Deletion

Types of data that may be deleted on request

Depending on your request and the data associated with your account, deletion may include:

  • Stored subscription or entitlement records linked to your app account.
  • Google Play account or subscription identifiers stored by the app for access verification, where applicable.
  • Other backend records linked to your account that are no longer required for app operation, security, or legal obligations.

Data that may be kept

Some limited records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, security, payment-dispute, or regulatory purposes. Such information will be retained only for as long as necessary for those purposes or as required by applicable law or the relevant payment platform.

Data that is no longer required will be deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

Your account remains active

A data deletion request made through this page does not automatically delete your Anxious Attachment Companion account.

If you want your entire account deleted, use our separate account deletion page .

Google Play subscriptions

Deleting app data does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. If you want to stop future subscription renewals, cancel the subscription separately through your Google Play account.

Verification of requests

We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing a backend data deletion request. This helps protect users against unauthorized deletion of their data.
