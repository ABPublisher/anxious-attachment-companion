Privacy Policy for Anxious Attachment Companion

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Anxious Attachment Companion is published and operated by AB Publisher LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Anxious Attachment Companion mobile application and related services.

Anxious Attachment Companion is designed for education, self-reflection, relationship wellness, and healthier communication. It is not therapy, medical or mental-health care, diagnosis, treatment, emergency assistance, or crisis support.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Your private reflections are primarily stored locally on your device. This may include:

  • Emotional check-ins
  • Trigger records
  • Assessment results
  • Message drafts
  • Follow-up records
  • Other reflections and app history you choose to save

These locally stored reflections are not synchronized to a cloud account and are not shared with other users. They may be permanently lost if you uninstall the app, clear its storage, clear applicable browser or WebView data, change devices without transferring local data, or otherwise remove the app's local data.

2. Account Information

If you create an account, information required for authentication and account access is processed remotely. This may include your email address, authentication information, account identifier, and information needed to maintain your signed-in session.

Your private check-ins, trigger records, assessments, and saved personal reflections are separate from your account information and are not stored in your account for cloud synchronization or recovery.

3. Subscription and Purchase Information

Anxious Attachment Companion may offer free trials, subscriptions, or other paid access. On Android, purchases and subscriptions may be processed through Google Play.

We may process or store limited subscription-access information, such as your account identifier, subscription status, purchase or subscription identifiers, entitlement status, and expiration information, when needed to verify whether Premium features should be available to you.

Payment providers process payment transactions under their own privacy policies. AB Publisher LLC does not receive or store your full payment-card details from Google Play transactions.

4. AI-Assisted Message Rewriting

Anxious Attachment Companion includes an optional AI-assisted feature, currently presented as “Make This Calmer”.

When you intentionally use this feature, the message draft you submit is transmitted through a server-side connection to an external AI service so that a calmer version of the message can be generated.

The AI service provider may process the text you submit in accordance with its own technical and data-handling practices. Do not submit information that you do not want processed by an external AI service.

Simply writing or saving a draft locally does not automatically submit it to the AI service. Submission occurs when you choose to use the AI rewriting feature.

5. Sensitive Content and Safety

The AI-assisted rewriting feature may apply safety handling when submitted text suggests self-harm, violence, abuse, or immediate danger. Such content may be handled differently from an ordinary message-rewriting request.

The app is not an emergency or crisis service. If you are in immediate danger or experiencing a crisis, contact appropriate local emergency services or a qualified crisis-support service.

6. Technical Information

When online features are used, service providers involved in authentication, subscription verification, hosting, security, and AI processing may automatically receive limited technical information such as IP address, device or browser information, request metadata, timestamps, and technical logs needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot their services.

7. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed for purposes including:

  • Authenticating your account
  • Providing access to app features
  • Verifying subscription and Premium entitlement status
  • Processing AI-assisted message-rewriting requests you initiate
  • Maintaining security and preventing misuse
  • Troubleshooting technical problems
  • Responding to support requests
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

8. Sharing of Information

AB Publisher LLC does not sell your private relationship reflections or personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers only where needed to operate particular features of the app. These categories may include:

  • Authentication and backend service providers
  • Google Play for Android purchases and subscriptions
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers
  • AI service providers when you intentionally use an AI feature
  • Security and technical service providers

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or valid governmental request.

9. No Public Profiles or Social Sharing

The app does not provide a public social feed, public user profiles, or a feature that shares your private reflections with other users.

10. Data Retention

Locally stored reflections remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's local storage, or uninstall the app.

Account and subscription-related information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide account access, verify subscriptions, maintain security, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

Following a valid deletion request, eligible remotely stored information will be deleted or anonymized as appropriate. Limited records may be retained where necessary for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, security, payment-dispute, or regulatory purposes, and will be removed or anonymized when they are no longer required.

11. Deleting Your Data and Account

You can remove your locally stored check-ins, triggers, assessment, and other locally stored reflections from within the app by going to Settings and choosing Clear all data. This removes those local records from the device without deleting your account.

Because private reflections are stored locally rather than synchronized to our servers, AB Publisher LLC generally cannot remotely retrieve or delete those local reflections for you.

If you want to keep your account but request deletion of eligible remotely stored account-associated data, use our data deletion page: Request data deletion .

If you want to delete your account and associated remotely stored account records, you can use the Request account deletion option in the app's Settings section or use our public account deletion page: Request account deletion .

You may also contact support@abpublisherllc.com from the email address associated with your account. For account deletion, use the subject line “Anxious Attachment Companion - Account Deletion Request”. We may take reasonable steps to verify account ownership before completing a deletion request. Never send us your password.

Deleting app data or deleting your account does not by itself cancel an active Google Play subscription. Google Play subscriptions should be managed or cancelled through Google Play subscription management.

12. Data Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the service to protect information processed through online features. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Local device storage is also subject to the security of your own device. You should protect your device using appropriate security measures.

13. Children's Privacy

Anxious Attachment Companion is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children or minors. If we learn that personal information has been collected in a manner that violates applicable children's privacy laws, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

14. Third-Party Services

Features of the app may depend on third-party services, including authentication, hosting, payment, subscription verification, and AI processing services. Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

15. International Processing

Online service providers may process information in countries other than the country in which you live. Where applicable, such processing is subject to the provider's safeguards and applicable data-protection laws.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its features, applicable law, or our data-handling practices change. The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when material revisions are made.
