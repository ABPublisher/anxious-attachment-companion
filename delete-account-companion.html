Delete Your Anxious Attachment Companion Account

Anxious Attachment Companion is published and operated by AB Publisher LLC.

You may request deletion of your account and associated account data at any time.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your Anxious Attachment Companion account.
  2. Send the request to support@abpublisherllc.com.
  3. Use the subject line: Anxious Attachment Companion - Account Deletion Request.
  4. State clearly that you want your Anxious Attachment Companion account and associated account data deleted.

Do not send your password. We will never ask you to provide your account password in an account deletion request.

Request Account Deletion

Data deleted with your account

After we verify and process your request, account-related information under our control may be deleted, including:

  • Your Anxious Attachment Companion authentication account.
  • Your account email address associated with the app.
  • Stored subscription or entitlement records linked to your app account.
  • Google Play account or subscription identifiers stored by Anxious Attachment Companion for verifying access, where applicable.
  • Other backend records linked specifically to your Anxious Attachment Companion account that are no longer required.

Private reflections stored on your device

Your check-ins, trigger records, assessments, message drafts, history, and similar private reflections are primarily stored locally on your device rather than in your online account.

To remove locally stored app data, you may use the app's available data-clearing controls, clear the app's storage through your device settings, or uninstall the app.

Information that may be retained

Certain limited records may be retained when necessary for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, security, payment-dispute, or regulatory purposes. Such information is retained only for as long as necessary for those purposes or as required by applicable law or the relevant payment platform.

Information that is no longer required for these purposes will be deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

Google Play subscriptions

Important: Deleting your Anxious Attachment Companion account does not automatically cancel a subscription managed by Google Play.

If you have an active Google Play subscription, cancel it separately through your Google Play account to prevent future renewal charges. Google may retain transaction and subscription records according to Google's own policies and legal obligations.

Processing your request

We may contact you to verify that you own the account before completing the deletion request. This helps protect users against unauthorized deletion of their accounts.
